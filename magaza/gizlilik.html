Privacy Policy

Don't Mess with the Necromancer · Last updated: October 11, 2026

In short

The game does not collect personal data, does not connect to the internet and does not ask you to create an account.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, stars, settings and language choice are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Ads and purchases

This version has no ads. If optional rewarded ads are added later, they will be served by Google AdMob, which may process device information such as the advertising ID under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). On iOS you will be asked for permission first. This policy will be updated at that time.

In-app purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
