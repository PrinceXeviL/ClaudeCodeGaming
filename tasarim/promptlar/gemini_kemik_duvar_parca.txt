Image 1 shows the art style of my dark gothic cartoon necromancer game (thick dark outlines, rich muted colors, bone white with green necromantic glow). Image 2 is my old "Bone Wall" spell, which I want to replace.

New design: the Bone Wall is made of SEPARATE giant bone pieces that each burst out of the ground on their own and stand side by side across the road, like a row of monstrous fangs. Draw a sheet of 10 such SEPARATE pieces, each one standing upright and complete from its tip down to a rough, jagged, broken base (as if the rest is still buried underground), seen from the front with a slight top-down 3/4 view, so the same piece looks right whatever direction the road goes:
1) a huge curved fang tooth, slightly yellowed, with a crack
2) another huge fang tooth, straighter, chipped tip
3) a tall sharpened bone spike, like a stake carved from a femur
4) a pair of curved rib bones crossing each other
5) a giant claw-like curved bone hook
6) a thick jagged bone shard with a smaller shard beside it
7) a monstrous molar tooth with three roots pointing up like spikes
8) a twisted horn-like bone spike with a faint green rune glowing on it
9) a short wide jawbone fragment with three teeth
10) a slim tall needle-like bone spike
All pieces are bone white to old ivory with dark brown cracks, a little dark earth stuck to their lower part, and a very faint green glow in the cracks. All pieces have about the same height except 9 (shorter) and 10 (taller); each is about twice as tall as it is wide.

Arranged in one or two clean rows, each piece centered in its own area with plenty of empty magenta space around it, nothing touching. Plain flat magenta (#FF00FF) background everywhere, no ground, no shadows on the background, no text, no letters, no labels. 16:9 image.
