Character design sheet for a mobile tower defense game, in EXACTLY the same hand-painted dark cartoon art style as the characters in the second image (thick dark outlines, rich colors, slightly exaggerated proportions). The first image is the OLD design of this character: keep only his role and his red-crimson color mood, do NOT copy the skull face, the hood or the scythe.

The character: MORTIMER, a grumpy old DARK ELF necromancer who only wants to drink his coffee in peace at home, and is furious that an empire keeps attacking his tower. He must look nothing like the Grim Reaper: NO skull face, NO hood over the head, NO scythe.
Face: dark elf with ash-grey / lilac skin, long pointed ears, long white hair, sharp aristocratic features, heavy frowning eyebrows, grumpy tired expression with dark circles under the eyes, faint glowing green eyes. Thin and tall.

Draw FOUR variations side by side, full body, front 3/4 view, same face and body, different outfit. Each outfit mixes a MAJESTIC necromancer robe with cozy AT-HOME clothing:
1) A grand deep-crimson velvet robe with gold and bone embroidery and a tall stiff collar, worn over striped pajamas, fluffy black slippers, holding a steaming coffee mug in one hand and a skull-topped staff in the other.
2) A dark purple ceremonial robe with a bone shoulder mantle and a long train, a knitted woolly scarf around the neck, wool socks with house sandals, reading glasses on his nose, a coffee mug.
3) A black and emerald-green robe with glowing green runes and silver bone clasps, a plush bathrobe belt tied around the waist, a sleeping cap with a little skull pom-pom, a coffee mug.
4) A crimson and black royal robe with a fur-lined cape and a small bone crown, a comfy cardigan underneath, slippers shaped like little skulls, a coffee mug and an open spell book.

Rules:
- Same character in all four (same face, hair, height); only the outfit changes.
- Funny but cool: he should look powerful AND like he just got out of bed.
- No smoke, no fog, no aura, no glow halo around him; light only in the eyes, runes and staff. No crosses.
- Plain flat white background, no ground, no text, no labels. Clear space between the four figures.
